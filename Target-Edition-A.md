# Target - Edition A

## Decision and initial view

Provisional action: watch/defer. The proposed user is a buy-side team with no current position in Target common stock (NYSE: TGT), using USD, an October 19, 2026 valuation date, and a proposed 12-24 month decision horizon. The working hypothesis is that recurring operating performance can recover modestly, while capital spending and competition limit the cash-flow benefit. No completed valuation range or price advantage has been established.

## Reported evidence anchors

- **2025 financial history:** Fiscal year ended January 31, 2026: net sales $104.780bn; adjusted operating margin 4.6%; total cash-flow-statement D&A $3.134bn; capex $3.727bn. [S1]

- **2026 update:** Q2 sales growth was 5.3%. August 19 guidance indicated full-year sales growth around 5% and operating margin around 6%, including about 90bp of tariff-refund benefit. [S2]

- **Normalization issue:** The Q2 release includes a $994m pretax tariff refund. Treat a repeat of that benefit as unproven, rather than building it into recurring margins. [S2]

## Research and model plan

Build five-year articulated statements and derive FCFF from them; estimate WACC and a sustainable terminal period; complete the cash/debt/other-claims bridge and diluted share definition. Freeze a peer policy before selecting peers, then compare at least one enterprise and one equity multiple. Explain disagreement across methods. Test causal low/base/high cases, WACC-growth sensitivity, reverse DCF, and one-at-a-time operating sensitivities using alternative justified ranges. No scenario values have been calculated in this baseline.

Planned checks: reconcile reported and normalized base-year figures; test yearly balance-sheet and cash articulation, the equity bridge, DCF boundaries, and multiple definitions; reconcile a course known-answer case; independently check a material source; preserve a highest-risk failure test and clean-environment run. Complete the separate protected changed-input record under its AI-off rules. These are planned checks, not completed results.

## Monitoring and action conditions

Proposed review triggers: reconsider growth after two consecutive reports with non-positive year-on-year comparable sales; reconsider margin if updated recurring margin guidance falls below 5.0%; defer any action while source, accounting, or equity-bridge checks remain unresolved. An initiation proposal requires a validated range, verified market price, and an explicitly justified entry condition.
