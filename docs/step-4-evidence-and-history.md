## Balance-sheet conventions and remaining judgment

- **Leases:** August 1 noncurrent operating lease liabilities are 3,332; total current and noncurrent operating liabilities are 3,733 (N13), implying 401 current within accrued liabilities. The FY2025 10-K Note 19 reports finance lease liabilities included in debt: 131 current and 1,982 noncurrent at January 31 (N14/N15); updated August components remain to be identified. Recommended starting approach: keep operating lease rent in operating expense and do not separately deduct operating lease liabilities unless operating earnings, FCFF, discount rate and comparable EVs are consistently recast. Deduct finance lease obligations once if included in interest-bearing debt. Review and document before modeling; an alternative capitalized approach is acceptable only with a complete corresponding recast.
- **Cash and debt:** August 1 reported cash and cash equivalents 5,411; current debt and borrowings 1,136 and long-term 14,221. These are reported August balances, not October 19 balances. Identify any restricted or required operating cash from the notes before adding only eligible excess cash in the bridge; the source does not establish that the entire balance is surplus. Assess pension/other non-common claims, deferred tax and lease components individually; don't assume missing claims or restrictions are zero. The 10-Q reports 1,000 of debt repaid in April, already reflected in August, and an August 4,000 undrawn credit facility replacing previous facilities; capacity is not outstanding debt. The September 23 declaration is 1.16 per share, payable December 1 to November 11 holders; it is a future payment, not proof of cash paid by October 19. Other intervening financing, repurchases, seasonal operating movements, settlement of claims, and actual October balances remain unverified. Use a sourced roll-forward or clearly labeled stale-balance approximation with sensitivity later, not a fabricated balance.
- **Shares:** August 1 common issued and outstanding were 454,291,461, compared with 452,840,187 at January 31, per the Q2 balance-sheet footnote (N08/N09). Recommend starting with latest period-end shares and separately estimating valuation-date dilution from outstanding awards, exercise proceeds and cutoff price using appropriate methods. Report historical diluted weighted-average EPS shares as a different measure. As-of October issuance/repurchases and award terms require evidence; avoid double-counting diluted awards or using a weighted average as an exact current count.
- **Operating working capital:** include inventory, operating receivables and other identified operating current assets, less trade payables and operational accruals; exclude cash and interest-bearing debt. Other current assets and accruals include mixed components, so separate financing/tax/other non-operating amounts from notes and disclose unresolved classifications. Inventory was 12,304 at FY2025 year-end versus 13,249 at August 1, 2026; payables were 12,622 versus 13,306. Comparative prior August inventory 12,881 versus prior January 12,740 shows differing seasonal and year effects. These observations indicate that a single annual ratio can miss seasonality; they do not establish a seasonal model or an October balance. Investigate quarterly patterns and supplier financing before setting assumptions.
