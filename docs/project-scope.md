## Units and share basis

Use U.S. dollars (USD). Enter and display financial-statement amounts and enterprise/equity values in **USD millions**, except where a source explicitly uses another scale; convert and label the transformation. Express valuation shares in **million shares**, and per-share outputs and quoted price in **USD per common share**. Rates use percentages with the base and period specified. Keep calculation precision internally and round only for presentation. Do not confuse USD millions with share millions.

The proposed valuation denominator is the estimated **diluted share count as of the valuation date**, in millions, consistent with the equity bridge and treatment of dilutive instruments. This is distinct from historical diluted weighted-average shares used for reported EPS. Actual share counts, methods, instrument treatment, and date adjustments remain to be sourced and reviewed.

- Use evidence publicly available **by 4:00 p.m. U.S. Eastern Time on October 19, 2026**, the intended price timestamp and information cutoff. The proposed price is the official NYSE regular-session closing price for TGT on that date, not an intraday, after-hours, adjusted historical close, or later quote. Record financial period end, public release timestamp/date, market quote timestamp (with time zone), and retrieval date as separate fields. If a source supplies only a publication date and time matters, verify availability by the cutoff or leave it pending.
