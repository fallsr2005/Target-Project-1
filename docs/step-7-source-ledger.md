# WACC and valuation evidence ledger

Research and retrieval date October 19, 2026; cutoff October 19, 2026 at 4 p.m. Eastern. These items combine sourced observations and separately labeled assumptions, not a validated WACC.

| ID | Input, unit and date | Evidence and status |
|---|---|---|
| W01 | 5.09%, annual 10-year Treasury par yield, October 16 | [U.S. Treasury daily par yield curve](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value=2026), October 16 row, 10-year column; prior-business-day proxy. October 19's 5.15% observation is not used because the public availability timestamp relative to 4 p.m. was not established. Treasury's par yield is not a zero-coupon yield. |
| W02 | 4.20%, annual implied equity risk premium as of October 1 | [NYU Stern, Damodaran data page](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/home.htm), trailing-12-month adjusted-payout variant. The page identifies October 1; the specific archival publication timestamp was not captured, so point-in-time availability is qualified. ERP is a market estimate, not guaranteed return; it was estimated with a different risk-free observation. |
| W03 | Levered beta 1.00, dimensionless | Pure provisional assumption; no verified as-of regression or point-in-time provider beta was available. Proposed methodology: regress weekly split/dividend-adjusted Target returns on a broad-market total-return index using five years ending no later than cutoff, document intercept, frequency, missing dates, sample and leverage. Do not substitute a later provider beta. |
| W04 | 6.0% pretax marginal borrowing rate | Provisional estimate, not a trade or a sourced Target yield. Q2 10-Q reports A2/A credit ratings and the 2025 10-K Note 17 reports historical bond coupons; those coupons are not the cutoff marginal cost. Obtain a dated issuer bond yield or credit spread and comparable Treasury tenor. |
| W05 | 24% tax shield | Assumed full interest deductibility with sufficient taxable income, consistent with the Step 6 operating-tax proxy. The [2025 10-K Note 20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/27419/000002741926000016/tgt-20260131.htm) reports actual income-tax components, not an established perpetual marginal shield. Interest limits and losses remain open. |
| W06 | 80% equity / 20% debt | Illustrative target weights, not a measured historical market capital structure. The official close remains disputed. August 1 balance-sheet debt and its filing-note fair-value estimate are stale and differ; book debt is used only in the bridge as a carrying-value proxy. Do not call book equity a market-equity weight. Replace with dated market equity and debt values when verified. |
| W07–W08 | 2% terminal growth, 12% after-tax incremental return on invested capital | Assumptions; Edition A supplies the 2% starting hypothesis, not evidence it is sustainable. 12% is not Target's verified marginal return. Require 0 ≤ growth < incremental return, calculate reinvestment as growth / incremental return, and disclose that this simplified perpetual steady state is not backed by a detailed terminal asset schedule. |
| W09 | August 1 common shares 454.291461 million; incremental proxy 2.2 million | [Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/27419/000002741926000042/tgt-20260801.htm) p. 3 and p. 1. The 2.2 million is the rounded difference between Q2 weighted-average diluted 456.6 and basic 454.4 million; it is only a placeholder for valuation-date award dilution, not a current award count. Verify award terms, vesting, share movements and a point-in-time treasury-stock calculation. |
| W10 | Other non-operating assets and claims unresolved | Q2 10-Q and FY2025 10-K notes. The provisional partial bridge does not quantify pension, other non-common claims, unclassified assets or other adjustments; null means unknown, not zero. An equity amount calculated with these omitted is illustrative and incomplete. |
| W11 | Official TGT close | Pending: provider prices conflict, and official auction value and adjustment definition remain unverified. No price, target upside or market-implied result is calculated by default. |

Every source cited above was publicly dated before the cutoff except where timestamp uncertainty is explicitly noted. The model uses annualized nominal USD rates. Its price and beta are not taken from a current provider snapshot.
