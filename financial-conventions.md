## Open implementation decisions

The proposed stub-plus-five-full-years convention makes the valuation cover more than five calendar years from the valuation date; document the resulting terminal date and retain all required five-year statements. The remaining FY2026 period may contain seasonality that makes a simple time-prorated annual amount misleading. The treatment of the unreported elapsed portion, fiscal-year end dates, partial-year discount day count, as-of cash/debt/share roll-forward, operating cash threshold, lease policy, operating tax rate and equity dilution need sourced evidence and student review before implementation. No amounts, price or valuation results are calculated here.
